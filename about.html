About K44TER

K44TER

ARTIST / PRODUCER / MIXING & MASTERING ENGINEER

I’m K44TER, an independent artist, producer and engineer from Cambodia. I make music, produce, mix and master my own records, and build a visual world around it.

My sound blends rage, emo, ambient and trap — dark, emotional and experimental, with my own perspective from Phnom Penh’s underground scene.
